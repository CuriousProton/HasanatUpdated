## 📋 Step-by-Step Guide to Publish on Google Play

### Step 1: Create Google Play Developer Account

1. Go to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Pay the **one-time registration fee of $25 USD**
4. Complete your developer profile

### Step 2: Create Your App

1. In Google Play Console, click **"Create app"**
2. Fill in the details:
   - **App name**: Hasanat Tracker
   - **Default language**: English (or your preferred language)
   - **App or game**: App
   - **Free or paid**: Free
   - **Declarations**: Check all required boxes
3. Click **"Create app"**

### Step 3: Complete Store Listing

Go to **"Store presence" > "Main store listing"** and fill in:

#### App Details:
- **Short description** (80 characters max):
  ```
  Track your daily good deeds (hasanat) - Salah, Quran, Charity, Dhikr, Fasting & Kindness
  ```

- **Full description** (4000 characters max):
  ```
  Hasanat Tracker - Your Daily Good Deeds Companion

  Track and record your daily Islamic good deeds (hasanat) with this beautiful and intuitive app. Stay motivated with authentic hadiths and Quranic verses about the rewards of your actions.

  ✨ Features:

  🕌 Salah Tracker
  - Track your five daily prayers
  - Color-coded prayer cards based on time of day
  - Authentic hadiths about prayer rewards
  - Track congregation prayers

  📖 Quran Tracker
  - Record verses and surahs you've read
  - Calculate rewards (10 hasanat per letter)
  - Special rewards for specific surahs
  - Comprehensive hadith collection about Quran reading

  💰 Charity Tracker
  - Log your charitable acts
  - Learn about 700x multiplication of rewards
  - Multiple ways to give charity

  ✨ Dhikr Counter
  - 12 authentic dhikr options
  - Vibration feedback on each count
  - Milestone celebrations
  - Specific hadiths for each dhikr
  - Recommended recitation counts

  ☀️ Fasting Tracker
  - Track your fasts
  - Learn about fasting rewards
  - Special days (Arafah, Ashura, etc.)

  👥 Kindness Tracker
  - Record acts of kindness
  - 360 daily charity opportunities
  - Authentic hadiths about kindness

  📊 Features:
  - Beautiful, modern UI
  - Daily hasanat summary
  - Motivational notifications
  - All hadiths verified from authentic sources (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, etc.)
  - No ads, completely free
  - Privacy-focused (data stored locally)

  Start your journey of tracking good deeds today!
  ```

#### Graphics Required:
- **App icon**: 512 x 512 px (you have `assets/icon.png`)
- **Feature graphic**: 1024 x 500 px (create this)
- **Screenshots**: 
  - Phone: At least 2, up to 8 (1080 x 1920 px or similar)
  - Tablet (optional): 7" and 10" tablets

#### Categorization:
- **App category**: Lifestyle or Education
- **Tags**: Islamic, Prayer, Quran, Charity, Good Deeds, Muslim

### Step 4: Content Rating

1. Go to **"Content rating"**
2. Complete the questionnaire:
   - Category: Lifestyle/Education
   - No violence, no sexual content, no gambling
   - Religious content: Yes (Islamic app)
3. Submit for rating (usually takes a few hours)

### Step 5: Privacy Policy (REQUIRED)

You **must** have a privacy policy URL. Options:

1. **Create a simple privacy policy** (you can use a free service):
   - [Privacy Policy Generator](https://www.privacypolicygenerator.info/)
   - [Termly](https://termly.io/)

2. **Key points to include**:
   - App stores data locally on device
   - No data collection or sharing
   - No third-party analytics
   - No ads

3. **Host it**:
   - GitHub Pages (free)
   - Google Sites (free)
   - Your own website

4. Add the URL in **"App content" > "Privacy policy"**

### Step 6: App Access

1. Go to **"App access"**
2. Select: **"All functionality is available without restrictions"** (since your app doesn't require login)

### Step 7: Ads (if applicable)

1. Go to **"Ads"**
2. Select: **"No, my app does not contain ads"**

### Step 8: Data Safety

1. Go to **"Data safety"**
2. Fill out the form:
   - **Data collection**: No, we don't collect data
   - **Data sharing**: No, we don't share data
   - **Data security**: Data stored locally on device
   - **Deletion**: Users can clear app data

### Step 9: Upload Your App Bundle

1. Go to **"Production"** (or "Testing" > "Internal testing" for testing first)
2. Click **"Create new release"**
3. Upload your **AAB file**: `hasanat-tracker.aab`
4. Add **Release notes**:
   ```
   Initial release of Hasanat Tracker
   - Track daily good deeds (hasanat)
   - Salah, Quran, Charity, Dhikr, Fasting & Kindness tracking
   - Authentic hadiths and Quranic verses
   - Beautiful, intuitive interface
   ```
5. Click **"Review release"**

### Step 10: Review and Submit

1. Review all sections:
   - ✅ Store listing complete
   - ✅ Content rating done
   - ✅ Privacy policy added
   - ✅ App bundle uploaded
   - ✅ All required sections completed

2. Click **"Submit for review"**

### Step 11: Wait for Review

- **Review time**: Usually 1-3 days (can be up to 7 days)
- Google will review your app for policy compliance
- You'll receive email notifications about the status
